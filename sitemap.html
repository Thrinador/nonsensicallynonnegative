Research Blog & Monograph

Inside Karpelevič’s Proof (9-Stop Series)

  • Roadmap: Inside Karpelevič’s Proof Master architectural map, Theorem A & Theorem B statements, unified notation table, and proof ledger.
  • Stop 1 of 9 · Stochastic Matrices as Contracting Polygons Dmitriev–Dynkin duality $\lambda M \subseteq M$, row normalization, and why boundary eigenvalues cannot hide inside a polygon.
  • Stop 2 of 9 · Boundary Eigenvalues and Edge Contacts Minimal order $n$, vertex vs. interior-edge contacts, and why every transformed vertex $\lambda z_i$ must lie on $\partial M$.
  • Stop 3 of 9 · The Circular-Board Game Behind Karpelevič’s Proof Elementary edge slides $D_1(\gamma)$ and $D_2(\gamma)$ translated into a circular-board chip game that concentrates interior-edge contacts.
  • Stop 4 of 9 · Return Relations and the Characteristic Equation Collapsing $n-g$ pure vertex transitions into an $m \times m$ return matrix $R(\lambda)$ and extracting the characteristic product.
  • Stop 5 of 9 · Why Every Contact Parameter Must Be Equal Single-loop cycle decomposition and strict concavity of $\theta \mapsto \log|v(\theta)|$, forcing all contact parameters to coincide.
  • Stop 6 of 9 · The Two Canonical Boundary Equations Deriving Family I ($\lambda^\tau(\lambda^\ell - t)^m = (1-t)^m$) and Family II ($(\lambda^\ell - t)^m = (1-t)^m\lambda^\sigma$) and connecting them to Ito’s unified equation.
  • Stop 7 of 9 · Farey Fractions and the Two-Step Law Sector bounds, Bézout recurrence $qk - pf = 1$, the two-step restriction $s_i \in \{\ell, \ell+h\}$, and the Farey exponent dictionary.
  • Stop 8 of 9 · Cyclic Polygons and Explicit Boundary Matrices Cyclic polygons $C(\mu, h)$, monotone polynomial envelopes, and explicit $n \times n$ stochastic companion realizations.
  • Stop 9 of 9 · Induction on Order and the Final Region $K_n$ Farey mediant surgery from $K_{n-1}$ to $K_n$, eliminating non-adjacent arcs via $n_* \le n-1$, and closing the proof of Theorem A and Theorem B.
