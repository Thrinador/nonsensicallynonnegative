Character Table Spectracones & the Order-5 Symmetric NIEP

November 1, 2026

Recent work by Johnson & Paparella (2025) and Gershnik, Lewis, & Paparella (2026) showed that the character tables of finite groups act as ideal Perron similarities, turning the nonlinear symmetric eigenvalue realization problem into explicit systems of linear halfspaces. This interactive exploration tests how much of the order-five Symmetric NIEP can be carved out by finite group character tables alone—capturing nearly the entire two-dimensional Loewy–McDonald benchmark slice once reducible adjoined blocks are included, isolating the microscopic curved hyperbola gap that polyhedral cones cannot reach, and lifting the construction into the unsolved three-dimensional trace-zero frontier.
