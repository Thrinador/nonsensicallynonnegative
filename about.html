Research · Collaboration · Open Source

About the NIEP Research Hub

An open-source initiative dedicated to advancing research on spectral problems of nonnegative matrices through interactive software, open datasets, and rigorous theoretical surveys.

How You Can Help & Contribute

We warmly welcome contributions from mathematicians, computational scientists, developers, and students

Mathematics & Theory

Mathematicians & Researchers

  • Submit new spectral realizability criteria or sufficiency theorems.
  • Contribute counterexamples or edge-case spectra for dimensions $n \ge 5$.
  • Expand and peer-review articles in the Theory Wiki (e.g. RNIEP, SNIEP, Perron Similarities).
  • Suggest references, proofs, or landmark papers in modern inverse eigenvalue literature.
Software & Algorithms

Developers & Engineers

  • Optimize numerical realization algorithms (e.g., Riemannian conjugate gradient, Nelder-Mead).
  • Enhance WebGL 3D polytope rendering and high-DPI Canvas graphics.
  • Improve mobile touch interactions, responsiveness, and assistive accessibility.
  • Add matrix export formatters (Julia, SymPy, Octave, Rust ndarray).
Education & Community

Students & Educators

  • Test the interactive visualizers and report usability suggestions or bugs.
  • Design classroom tutorials, pedagogical walkthroughs, or problem sets.
  • Help proofread mathematical typography, LaTeX formulas, and explanations.
  • Contribute interesting test spectra and visualization presets.
